# Dialogue and subtitles

Transcribe recorded speech and export editable SRT/VTT files.

In GimmeStudio: **Help → Dialogue and subtitles**. Tool: `captions`.

## Create subtitles

1. Choose the audio or video asset in Dialogue & subtitles. For final delivery, choose the final timeline render so timestamps match the edit.
2. Click Transcribe locally and wait for the job. Whisper creates draft words and timings from the real audio.
3. Reload the project, open Edit captions and listen while correcting text and timing.
4. Each line uses: start seconds | end seconds | spoken text. Example: 0.4 | 2.8 | Every sandcastle starts with one small try.
5. Mark the captions reviewed only after checking them, then save. Download SRT or VTT from the caption record.
6. Import the subtitle file into your publishing or editing tool and check its alignment there.

## Keep versions aligned

Re-editing the video can change every later timestamp. Transcribe or retime the new final render after changes. Subtitle sidecars are separate files; this interface does not automatically burn captions into the video pixels.
