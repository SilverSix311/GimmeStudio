# Timeline and sound

Turn selected takes into a local edit with transitions and audio layers.

In GimmeStudio: **Help → Timeline and sound**. Tool: `timeline`.

## Build the edit

1. Open Timeline & sound. Add the image and video assets you want in sequence.
2. Reorder clips and set source trims or still durations. Adjust clip audio gain, brightness or saturation where needed.
3. Choose output resolution and frame rate. Set the transition and transition length to suit the pacing.
4. Add audio layers for dialogue, music or ambience. Set source in/out, timeline offset and volume. Enable ducking when appropriate, then listen to the result.
5. Save timeline and render. The new preview appears in Assets and on the timeline page after reload.
6. Play the complete edit, checking transitions, audio joins, timing and final duration. Adjust and render a new version as needed.

## Professional transitions

Use transitions to support the scene. Fades can bridge time or atmosphere, but a clean motivated cut can be more natural between continuous actions. Avoid masking a continuity problem with a long dissolve. Rendering at 1080p changes the output canvas, not the original quality of a low-resolution draft.
